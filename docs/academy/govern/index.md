## Govern

**This track is for a team lead or architect who already has automation and has to make it safe.** The scripts exist and they mostly work. The question now is whether you'd let them run against production unattended, and whether you could prove to an auditor that you should.

<div class="grid cards" markdown>

-   ### Production-Grade Principles

    Device identity, pre-flight checks, blast radius, drift, idempotency, rollback, audit, human-in-the-loop, and knowing when not to automate. Plus a charter, control matrix and 30/60/90-day roadmap for running it as a programme.

    [Production-Grade Principles](../../foundation/production-grade-network-automation-principles/index.md)

    A coded control catalogue you can cite, the method for assessing yourself against it, and the Python engineering bar.

-   ### Reference Architectures

    Designs with the reasoning behind every boundary, starting with Trusted Automation Execution: Zero Trust applied to the automation that holds your most privileged credentials.

-   ### Governed AI for Network Operations

    How to give an AI agent access to the network without giving it a shell: risk classification, tool design, contracts, and a review checklist.
