## The Nautomation Prime Academy

The Academy is everything Nautomation Prime publishes for free: tutorials from your first script to expert patterns, a seven-chapter capstone project, the production-grade principles and standards we assess against, and line-by-line deep dives into real tools. It is organised by who you are, not by topic.

## Three tracks

<div class="grid cards" markdown>

-   ### Learn

    **For a network engineer who wants to write Python.** Beginner tutorials, intermediate techniques, the Capstone, then expert patterns, in that order.

    [Go to Learn](learn/index.md)

-   ### Govern

    **For a team lead or architect who already has automation and has to make it safe.** Production-grade principles, a coded control catalogue, reference architectures, and governed AI.

    [Go to Govern](govern/index.md)

-   ### See it done

    **For someone deciding whether to hire.** Deep dives into finished tools, the PRIME Framework that governs delivery, and the services built on both.

    [Go to See it done](see-it-done/index.md)
