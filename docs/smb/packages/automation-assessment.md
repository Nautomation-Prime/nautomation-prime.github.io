## Automation Opportunity Assessment

### The Lowest-Risk Way to Start

Before you invest in any automation, you need to know two things:

1. **Which processes are actually worth automating?**
2. **What's the realistic return on that investment?**

Guessing gets expensive. Automating the wrong thing wastes budget and demoralises the team. This assessment gives you the data to make an informed decision — before any development work begins.

---

## What You Get

A short, focused engagement designed to answer the key question: *where will automation deliver the fastest, most meaningful impact for your team?*

**The Process:**

1. **Scoping Call (45 minutes)** — A remote call with Christopher to understand your environment, your team's current workload, and which manual processes are causing the most pain or risk.

2. **Background Analysis (2–3 business days)** — We review the information gathered and build realistic time-savings and ROI estimates for each process.

3. **Written Report** — Delivered as a PDF within 3–5 business days of the scoping call. Clear, actionable, and suitable for sharing with budget holders.

---

## What the Report Covers

For each process assessed, the report includes:

- **Current state description** — how long it takes, how often it runs, how many engineers are involved
- **Automation feasibility score** — how straightforward is this to automate given your environment?
- **Estimated time savings** — realistic hours saved per month, not inflated figures
- **Annual cost equivalent** — based on an indicative engineer day rate (you can substitute your own)
- **Risk assessment** — what could go wrong if this process continues to be manual?
- **Recommended automation approach** — what tool, what package, what level of effort
- **Priority ranking** — which to tackle first based on ROI, feasibility, and risk reduction
