This Privacy Policy explains what personal information is collected when you use this website and how it is handled.

## 1. Data Collected

This website itself does **not**:

- Collect personal data  
- Use cookies  
- Use analytics or tracking  
- Store logs for profiling or marketing purposes  

When you contact us via email, we receive only the information you choose to include in your message. This includes applications submitted to the [Nautomation Prime Foundation's charity services offer](../foundation/charity-services.md), where we ask for an organisation name, registration number, contact details, and a description of the request.

If you join our mailing list, we collect the email address you enter. See [Mailing List](#4-mailing-list) below.

## 2. How Your Data Is Processed

Email communications are processed through our current business email service provider.

Where third-party email infrastructure is used, processing is limited to what is necessary to deliver, route, and store email communications securely.

## 3. Purpose of Processing

We use the information you provide in email communications _solely_ for:

- Responding to your enquiry  
- Discussing potential work or collaboration  
- Following up about questions you have raised  

We do **not** use your data for marketing, sharing with third parties, or any unrelated purpose. The one exception is the mailing list, which you join separately and which is described below.

## 4. Mailing List

The sign-up form on Academy pages ("Get notified when the book ships") is provided by **Kit** ([kit.com](https://kit.com)), an email newsletter service.

- **What is collected:** the email address you enter, which is sent to and stored by Kit. Kit also records when and through which form you signed up.
- **Loading the form:** pages showing the form load it from Kit's servers, so Kit receives the standard information your browser sends with any web request, such as your IP address and browser type.
- **What it is used for:** occasional emails about the Nautomation Prime book (including when it is published) and new Academy material. Your address is not sold or shared with anyone else.
- **Legal basis:** your consent, given when you sign up. You can withdraw it at any time.
- **Unsubscribing:** every email includes an unsubscribe link, or you can email [enquiries@nautomationprime.io](mailto:enquiries@nautomationprime.io) and ask to be removed.
- **Retention:** your address stays on the list until you unsubscribe or ask us to remove it.
- **Where it is stored:** Kit is based in the United States, so your email address is stored outside the UK. Kit's handling of your data is described in its own [privacy policy](https://kit.com/privacy).

## 5. Legal Basis

The legal bases for using your data are:

- **Legitimate interest** — to respond to your enquiry  
- **Consent** — which is provided when you contact us  

## 6. Data Retention

Email communications are kept only as long as necessary to handle your enquiry, unless ongoing collaboration or legal requirements necessitate longer retention. You may request deletion of your data at any time.
