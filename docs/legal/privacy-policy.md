This website does **not**:

- Sell your data or use it for advertising

We use the information you provide in email communications _solely_ for:

## 4. Mailing List

The sign-up form on the homepage and Academy pages ("Get notified when the book ships") is provided by **Kit** ([kit.com](https://kit.com)), an email newsletter service.

- **What is collected:** the email address you enter, which is sent to and stored by Kit. Kit also records when and through which form you signed up.
- **Loading the form:** pages showing the form load it from Kit's servers, so Kit receives the standard information your browser sends with any web request, such as your IP address and browser type.
- **What it is used for:** occasional emails about the Nautomation Prime book (including when it is published) and new Academy material. Your address is not sold or shared with anyone else.
- **Legal basis:** your consent, given when you sign up. You can withdraw it at any time.
- **Unsubscribing:** every email includes an unsubscribe link, or you can email [enquiries@nautomationprime.io](mailto:enquiries@nautomationprime.io) and ask to be removed.
- **Retention:** your address stays on the list until you unsubscribe or ask us to remove it.
- **Where it is stored:** Kit is based in the United States, so your email address is stored outside the UK. Kit's handling of your data is described in its own [privacy policy](https://kit.com/privacy).

## 5. Cookies and Analytics

When you first visit, a cookie-consent banner asks before anything optional is switched on. You can accept everything, reject everything, or choose each option yourself; none is ticked until you do. Until you accept analytics, the site loads none and sends nothing to Google.

- **Google Analytics (optional):** if you accept it, the site loads Google Analytics 4, which sets cookies (such as `_ga`) so it can tell visits apart. It records which pages are viewed, searches made with the site's search box, and general information such as browser, device type and approximate location. We use it only to see which pages are read, so we can improve them. The data is processed by Google, which may store it outside the UK; Google Analytics 4 does not log or store IP addresses.
- **GitHub (optional):** if you accept it, the site fetches public details of our GitHub organisation, shown next to the GitHub link in the header, from GitHub's servers.
- **Your choice:** the site remembers your choice in your browser's local storage, so the banner doesn't reappear on every page. You can change it at any time with the **Change cookie settings** link at the bottom of every page.
- **Kit:** pages with the mailing-list sign-up form load it from Kit's servers whether or not you accept analytics (see [Mailing List](#4-mailing-list)). Kit's scripts may set their own cookies, for example when you subscribe; these are covered by Kit's [privacy policy](https://kit.com/privacy).
